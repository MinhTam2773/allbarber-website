01 — What's included

Everything the shop runs on, in one place.

01

Online booking

Clients see real-time availability and book any barber 24/7, from any device. No more phone tag during a cut.

02

Walk-in queue

Walk-ins check in at a kiosk and see their wait. Booked clients and walk-ins share one fair, visible line.

03

Front desk & POS

Check clients in, take payment and manage the day's appointments from a single front-desk screen.

04

Staff & payroll

Build schedules, set availability and work out each barber's pay without a spreadsheet.

05

Reports that matter

Revenue, staff performance, top services and returning clients, updated as the day goes on.

06

Many locations, one account

Compare shops side by side and see every location's numbers from one place.
